# Workstream 4: Change Management & Enablement

> **Workstream Lead:** TBD | **Status:** 🔵 Planning | **Last Updated:** May 2026

---

## Purpose

Drive adoption of AI-native tools and processes across Fintech/Services through structured change management, targeted enablement, and sustained culture-building. Without this workstream, tool deployment and process redesign will stall at the boundaries of human behavior.

---

## Objectives

1. Assess organizational readiness for AI-native PDLC adoption
2. Design and deliver role-based AI enablement programs
3. Build an internal community of AI-native practitioners ("AI Champions")
4. Measure and accelerate adoption across Fintech/Services teams
5. Address resistance, skill gaps, and change fatigue proactively

---

## Change Model

This workstream uses a structured change framework (ADKAR-informed):

| Stage | Focus | Key Activity |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Awareness** | Why are we changing? | Leadership communications, program launch |
| **Desire** | Do people want to change? | Pilot successes, peer storytelling |
| **Knowledge** | Do people know how? | Role-based training, playbooks |
| **Ability** | Can people do it? | Hands-on workshops, AI Champions support |
| **Reinforcement** | Will the change stick? | Recognition, metrics, feedback loops |

---

## Audience Segments & Enablement Approach

| Audience | AI-Native Need | Enablement Format |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Engineers | AI coding, test gen, code review | Hands-on labs, peer learning |
| PMs / POs | AI-assisted discovery, PRDs | Workshop + self-guided prompting guides |
| QA / Test Engineers | AI test generation, regression prediction | Technical deep-dives |
| Engineering Managers | AI metrics, team coaching | Leadership briefings |
| Exec Leaders | Strategy, ROI, risk | Exec briefings, dashboards |

## Dependencies

- WS2 (Tooling): training content requires finalized tool decisions
- WS3 (Process Redesign): playbooks and workflows must be ready before enablement
- WS1 (Strategy): exec communications must be approved before launch comms

---

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| AI skill gaps are deeper than anticipated | Role-specific learning paths; external training partnerships |
| Change fatigue from multiple concurrent initiatives | Sequence and align with existing org change calendar |
| AI Champions burning out | Formal recognition, time allocation, and manager support |
| Training not reinforced on the job | Manager enablement; team-level adoption check-ins |
